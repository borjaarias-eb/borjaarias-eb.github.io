Smart Contrast Card

This card now features Dynamic Contrast Logic. If you pick a dark background, the side panel will automatically lighten itself. If you pick a light background, it will darken.

The "middle point" is calculated using the YIQ color space formula. This ensures that the visual weight of the side panel feels natural regardless of the hue or brightness you select.
