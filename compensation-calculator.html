Spain tax note

For qualifying Spanish dismissal severance, AEAT says the IRPF exemption is generally limited to the mandatory legal amount and capped at 180,000. In collective dismissals/ERE, the exempt reference can be the unfair-dismissal limit: 33 days per worked year, capped at 24 monthly payments.
